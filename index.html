About My Hobby & Process

How I Started

My passion for digital graphic design started from playing mobile football games and admiring custom player cards. I loved how events were visually presented in FC Mobile, which inspired me to create my own unique concepts.

Tools & Platforms Used

To craft these designs, I rely on several key tools: FC Forum for community assets, PicsArt for image editing and layering, RenderZ for player stats reference, and ChatGPT to outline content and brainstorm ideas.

Development Note: Upgraded to Firebase Firestore cloud database for real-time cross-device synchronization with optimized mobile image compression.
